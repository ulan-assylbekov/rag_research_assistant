## License and attribution

The ACL Anthology states that its materials published in or after 2016 are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The DetectGPT arXiv record also lists CC BY 4.0. Keep the source titles and canonical links above with the PDFs, and credit the paper authors when reusing the material. Third-party content inside an individual paper may have separate terms.

## Corpus scope

This is a small reference corpus for answering questions about AI-generated text detection research. It is not a labeled dataset for training or evaluating a text detector. The selected papers cover methods, multilingual and real-world evaluation, robustness, historical context, and limitations.
